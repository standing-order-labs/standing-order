# Security Policy

## Status

Standing Order has **not been audited**. Until a `v1.0.0` release announces an audit, treat all deployments as experimental.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Report privately using either:

- GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: **Security -> Report a vulnerability** on this repository, or
- Email **franklinrichard2007@Gmail.com**.

Include a description, impact, and a proof of concept (ideally a failing test). We aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours and to share a remediation plan within 14 days.

## Scope

In scope: contracts under `contracts/`. Out of scope: third-party dependencies (report upstream), and issues requiring a compromised subscriber or merchant key.

## Disclosure

We follow coordinated disclosure and will credit reporters who wish to be named.
